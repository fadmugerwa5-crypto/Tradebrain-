# TradeBrain 1.0.392 (v39.2) — patient Executor + a Watchtower that acts

Built on v39.1. Existing screens, brokers, key storage, live lock, daily-loss lock, uncertain-order pause, Radar and
the Android/Windows workflows are unchanged. The colony still starts disarmed.

## 1. Patient Executor
Why trades were still sold too soon in v39.1: the AI Executor could close a Deriv contract the moment it was at even
+$0.01 (the v39.1 guard only covered negative marks), with no minimum hold.

New rules (`src/lib/trade/exit-policy.ts`, all deterministic, all unit tested):
- Deriv: the Executor is not asked about a contract until it has been held for the larger of "Executor patience"
  (default 90 s) and 40% of the contract's life (5-minute contract = 120 s).
- Deriv: it is only asked when open profit is at least "Deriv: profit worth selling early" (default 35% of stake).
  Smaller profits, and every loss, run to expiry. Not asked in the last 30 s either.
- After the AI answers CLOSE, the same rules are checked again on the latest value. A stale answer cannot sell.
- The v39.1 emergency loss guard (-60% of stake) is kept, but it now also waits out the minimum hold.
- Binance/practice trades keep the candle-structure guard and also wait out the minimum hold (scalp 90 s, trader 30 min).
- The Executor prompt now defaults to HOLD and says dips and small profits are not reasons to close.
- Both numbers are editable per style in Intel > Settings (Executor patience, Deriv profit worth selling early).

## 2. Watchtower that can trade (through the colony, never around it)
Why it never traded in v39.1: it was display-only, it re-checked prices only while its tab was open, and the saved
"trigger" was just the price at the moment of the WAIT.

Now (`src/lib/trade/watchtower.ts`, `src/lib/hive/loop.ts`):
- A colony WAIT is saved only if: a direction is leaned on by at least 2 analysts, it is not a risk veto or an AI
  failure, and there is real structure. The trigger is the highest high (BUY) or lowest low (SELL) of the last 20
  completed candles; invalidation is the opposite edge of the last 10. Wicks do not count: a COMPLETED candle must
  close beyond the level (plus 0.1 ATR).
- The engine re-checks every saved opportunity on every 2 s heartbeat, tab open or not, armed or not. No AI calls.
- Statuses: MONITORING, APPROACHING_TRIGGER, READY_FOR_MASTER_REVIEW, EXECUTED, REJECTED, INVALIDATED, EXPIRED,
  PAUSED_DATA (stale/missing candles can never trigger).
- Confirmed trigger => the colony runs a fresh round on that one market with the original thesis as context. This is
  a full round: analyst vote, Risk Guard, Executor, sizing, broker checks. It can still say WAIT.
  It runs ahead of the normal scan but only when the colony is armed, the desk is not paused, there is room for another
  trade, the loss cooldown is over and that market is not already open.
- False breakouts (price back inside the level), extended moves (> 1.5 ATR past the level) and invalidations are handled.
  A WAIT after review needs a newer confirming candle; after 3 reviews without approval it is archived as REJECTED.
- Radar setups are still shown but are traded by Radar's own engine, not by the Watchtower.

## Tests
`npm test`: 175 passed, 0 failed here (25 new: exit policy, structure, save rules, dedupe, approaching, wick vs close,
BUY/SELL, false breakout, extension, invalidation, expiry, stale data, persistence, review scheduling, rejection).

## Not verified (be honest with yourself)
- `npm run typecheck` and `npm run build` could not be run here (dependencies cannot be installed). A reduced typecheck
  showed no new genuine errors, but only a real build proves it. Run both before trusting the APK.
- Nothing was run against Deriv or Binance. Test on Deriv demo first.
- A communication failure inside a Watchtower review is recorded as a normal WAIT review for now (the full
  message-trace system in the v39 brief is not built).
- None of this makes the strategy profitable. Early-exit patience trades smaller wins for fewer premature exits;
  only forward testing shows which is better for you.
