# TradeBrain v39.3 — AI-chosen size + Deriv multiplier desk

Overlay on the v39.2 source (patient Executor + Watchtower). Existing brokers, key storage, live lock, daily-loss lock,
uncertain-order pause, Watchtower, Radar and the Android/Windows workflows are untouched. The colony still starts disarmed.

## Why wins were $1 (cause)
- Deriv rise/fall pays a fixed amount below the stake, and the stake was 0.5% of equity (~$45 on $9,000). The best
  possible win was ~$40, early exits made it smaller, and a win could never be larger than a loss.

## 1. The Deriv desk trades multipliers (stop loss + take profit held by Deriv)
- New setting Intel > Settings > "Deriv trade type": Multiplier (default) or Rise / fall (old behaviour).
- The colony's stop and target become the contract's stop loss and take profit. Existing, already tested sizing
  (`sizeMultiplier`) works out the stake and multiplier so that the loss at the stop equals the chosen risk.
- A multiplier needs a real reward: reward must be at least 1.8x risk (target falls back to 2.5x).
- If Deriv offers no multiplier on a market, no order is sent (it does not silently fall back to rise/fall).
- Multipliers have no expiry. The Executor is only asked about closing once profit is >= 1x the risk and the
  minimum hold has passed; otherwise Deriv's stop and target decide. A time stop (default 30 min) closes an overstayer.

## 2. The AI chooses its own size, inside hard limits
- The Executor now answers with `riskPct` (percent of equity lost if the stop is hit) and is told the base and maximum.
- Code decides how much of that it gets (`src/lib/hive/conviction.ts`):
  - Base risk is 0.5% ($45 on $9,000). Hard max default 1.0% ($90). Both editable.
  - The AI can go above base only if confidence >= 70, at least 3 analysts agree, reward >= 2x risk AND the last trade
    was not a loss. The ceiling rises with confidence (70 -> base, 95 -> max).
  - Until 20 trades are recorded on the desk, the scale-up is limited to half.
  - Never bigger after a loss. Risk Guard can only shrink. No martingale.
  - Total risk across open trades is capped (default 3% of equity). A single stop can never exceed what is left of
    the daily loss budget.
  - Every cap that applied is written into the trade's receipt text.
- Example at 2.5x reward: base trade wins ~$112, a high-conviction trade at max wins ~$225; the loss is capped at the risk.

## Tests
`npm test`: 200 passed, 0 failed here (15 new: every sizing rule, parsing, a full colony round where the AI asks for
its own size and sees the limits, multiplier review/time stop, multiplier payoff maths).

## NOT verified

- Nothing was run against Deriv. Multiplier orders must be tested on the Deriv DEMO account first. Unknowns: Deriv's
  commission on multipliers, whether some fast synthetic indices stop out through a stop on a spike, and the market
  list that offers multipliers.
- The Journal will say "Deriv contract sold" for both a stop loss and a take profit (Deriv reports both as a sale).
- Not built: move-stop-to-breakeven after 1R (needs Deriv contract updates), AI-planned staged entries.
- None of this proves profitability. Bigger size multiplies losses as well as wins. Watch the average R per trade over
  100+ demo trades before raising the maximum.
